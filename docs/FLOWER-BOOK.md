# Tilly's flower book

My garden now has a ten-entry flower book with large original illustrations and a close-up 3D preview. Grow the four familiar flower toys — daisy, sunflower, tulip and moonflower — to remember them permanently. Each newly grown variety earns one star. Growing another of the same variety does not award another collection star, and clearing a bed does not remove its entry.

Each of the six distinct pairs of familiar flowers unlocks a special packet from Tilly: apricot daisy, coral sunflower, golden tulip, starlight daisy, twilight sunflower or moonlight tulip. The book shows both required flowers with grown marks, and Tilly explains each pair in a British recording. This is a gift for growing a collection, not a claim about real cross-breeding or colour genetics. The moonflower is explicitly described as a make-believe flower.

Unlocked packets are free, with one unused seed of each special variety supplied at a time. They can be replenished after planting, without a timer or random chance. Three button presses water a planted seed to maturity. The actual coloured models appear in both the close-up garden and the island; butterflies, bees and birds respond to their familiar flower families. Crops and pizza baskets retain their previous behaviour.

The garden now shows at most four owned seed types on a page. Page changes focus the first seed on that page. In the book, A inspects a flower and focuses its seed action, so moving to that action cannot accidentally choose an intervening flower. Taking a packet focuses an empty bed; a full garden instead keeps the seed and explains that it can be planted later. B cancels clearing and restores focus to the same bed. Returning from the book also restores useful focus and preserves the new instruction recording.

Existing mature beds migrate into the collection without retroactive star awards. Seeds are bounded to 99, collections to ten known IDs, and planting and watering validate plot bounds. Growing flowers does not change the school practice record or phonics knowledge.
