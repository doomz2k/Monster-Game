# A private record of practice

Start now includes a collapsed **Practice on this device** section. It shows questions finished in counting, addition, subtraction, repeating patterns, sound choice, word building and space activities. The recent list includes the actual sum, count, sound, word or space prompt.

Each result distinguishes a match on the first answer from a match after another try. Requested example replays and adult sound prompts are recorded separately. These are observations of game inputs: pictures, examples and spoken guidance remain available, and the results are not a school assessment. An adult sound prompt means the game asked for a grown-up model, not that a person or teacher provided or approved one.

Pizza toppings are three separate questions, with their correct arithmetic skill. Rover surveys join the counting record. A whole pizza still awards its usual single recipe reward, and a rover survey still awards one star. Deliveries, purchases, decorative play and demonstrations do not invent completed learning results.

The record starts with this update. Existing stars, quests, settings, inventory and phonics approval gates are preserved, and no earlier completion counters are treated as answers. Records stay in the existing browser save and its export/recovery copies. No new network service, analytics endpoint, identity field or timestamp was added.

The data holds cumulative per-skill totals, up to 80 recent question records and up to 12 completed outcomes per skill. Unfinished answers survive leaving and save/resume. Pressing confirm again on the same unchanged response adds nothing, and a completed question cannot be counted twice. Malformed imported data is bounded and unknown skills are ignored. Difficulty and parent-selected ranges are unchanged in this checkpoint; later adaptation can use actual observations rather than completion totals.
